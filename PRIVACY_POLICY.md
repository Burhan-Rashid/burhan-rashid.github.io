# Privacy Policy for My Workout Weights

**Last updated: 20 July 2026**

My Workout Weights ("the app") is provided by Briskly Labs ("we", "us"). This policy
explains what the app collects, why, and what control you have over it.

Contact for any privacy question or request: **brisklylabs@gmail.com**

---

## What we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work. There is no advertising SDK, no analytics
SDK, and no crash-reporting SDK in the app.

| What                                                                                                      | Why                                                                                        | When                       |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------- |
| **Email address**                                                                                         | To create and sign you into your account                                                   | On sign-up                 |
| **Name**                                                                                                  | Shown in the app; only if you choose Google Sign-In                                        | On sign-up                 |
| **Account ID**                                                                                            | Internal identifier that links your data to your account                                   | On sign-up                 |
| **Fitness data** — the exercises you log, the weights and optional rep counts, and the date of each entry | This is the core function of the app: storing your lifting history so you can see it later | Each time you log a weight |
| **Purchase status**                                                                                       | To know whether you've bought the Pro upgrade so we can unlock those features              | If you make a purchase     |

We do **not** collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers for
advertising, or any health data beyond the weights you type in yourself.

## How we use it

Your data is used only to operate the app: to authenticate you, to store and show your
workout history, and to unlock Pro features if you have purchased them. We do not sell
your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use it to build advertising
profiles.

## Who processes your data

We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf and are not permitted to
use it for their own purposes:

- **Supabase** — hosts the database and handles authentication. Your account and workout
  history are stored here. https://supabase.com/privacy
- **Google** — provides Google Sign-In (if you choose it) and Google Play Billing for
  purchases. https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **RevenueCat** — processes and validates your in-app purchase and tells the app whether
  your Pro upgrade is active. https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Payment card details are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store them.

## Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Your login session is stored on
your device using the operating system's secure storage. Database access is restricted by
row-level security rules so that your records are only readable by your own account.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account and workout history for as long as your account exists. When you
delete your account, the data is deleted as described below.

## Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app:

**Settings → Delete account → Delete forever**

This permanently and immediately erases your account, your logged weights, and any custom
exercises you created. It cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app, email **brisklylabs@gmail.com** from the address you
signed up with and ask for deletion. We will delete the account within 30 days and
confirm by reply.

Note: a Pro purchase is a record held by Google Play, not by us. Deleting your account
does not cancel or refund it, and you can restore it from the app's upgrade screen if you
sign up again. Refund requests go through Google Play.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete
the personal data we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Email
**brisklylabs@gmail.com** and we will respond within 30 days.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from
them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material
changes will be announced in the app.
